the approach

How I build every trip

Three things every itinerary I plan comes back to, no matter the destination.

Prioritize nature

Any trip with me includes getting to know a new place by visiting its parks, waterfront trails and colourful outdoor art spaces. Time spent outdoors is spiritual, healing, clarifying and is guaranteed to lead to lasting memories.

Live like a local

I know we're tourists but we're going to try not to be touristy. Resting somewhere safe, quiet and centrally located to where your adventure takes you is needed for reflection that will solidify the feeling of your experiences.

Travel flexibly

Slowing down and staying a while reduces stress if something changes. Use transportation pricing to determine your travel dates and fill everything else in from there. Remember to leave space on the calendar for local agency deals and unplanned adventures.

how I plan

Four values, every itinerary

Every itinerary I build gets run through the same four checks. If these match how you want to travel, we'll work well together.

  • Local over tourist Fewer landmarks, more of what the people who actually live there do on a normal Tuesday.
  • Slower travel Stay at least a week before moving on. Depth over a checklist of cities.
  • Safety, seriously Especially as a solo traveler myself, safety isn't an afterthought in any plan I build.
  • Affordability A great trip and a realistic budget aren't opposites. I plan for both.

Who this is for: aspiring digital nomads figuring out remote work abroad, solo adventurers who want to explore on foot, travelers tired of touristy itineraries, and burned-out professionals ready for a real change of pace.
