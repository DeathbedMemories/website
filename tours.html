Cannabis Walking Tours

When I'm not on the road, I lead small, relaxed walking tours around the city — cannabis-friendly, nature-forward, and built for people who want a slower, more social way to see the place they live (or are visiting).

how it works

What to expect

Small, easy groups

Solo, couples, or small groups of friends — a relaxed pace, a few stops, and plenty of time to actually enjoy the walk rather than rush through it.

Roughly 2 hours

A loop through parks, waterfront, or neighborhood routes, picked based on the season and what you're in the mood for.

Bring your own

I guide the route and the pace — you bring whatever you're comfortable consuming, and consume responsibly and legally for your province. I don't supply, sell, or handle cannabis as part of the tour.

Must be of legal cannabis consumption age in your province. Availability is seasonal — generally summer months, weather and travel schedule permitting.
